About the shop

Medieval Armour Shop sells medieval helmets, full suits of armour, swords, chainmail, shields, costumes and collector pieces — for historical reenactment, training, collectors and display.

Every product is listed with its price in euros. Orders are order requests: you place your order at checkout, and the shop confirms it and sends payment details by email. No payment is taken on this site.

The shop ships across Europe. Shipping costs and delivery times are to be confirmed by the store.
