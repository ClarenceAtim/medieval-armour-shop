Frequently asked questions

How do I order?

Add products to your cart and go to checkout. Fill in your name, email and delivery address, then place your order request. No payment is taken on this site.

How do I pay?

After you place an order request, the shop confirms your order and sends payment details by email. Payment methods are to be confirmed by the store.

How much is shipping, and how long does delivery take?

Shipping costs and delivery times are to be confirmed by the store. The shop gives you the shipping details by email when it confirms your order.

Can I return an item?

The returns policy is to be confirmed by the store. See the Returns page.

Are the swords sharp?

It depends on the product. Combat and practice swords are generally blunt (battle-ready for reenactment and training); decorative swords are for display. Check the product page — where the details list a blade type or edge, that is what the product is.

Are the product photos final?

Product photos are representative and may be updated by the shop. If you need exact photos of an item, ask the shop when you place your order.
